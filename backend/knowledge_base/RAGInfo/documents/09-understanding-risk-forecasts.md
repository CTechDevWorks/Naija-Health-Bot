## What does a malaria risk level or forecast for an area mean?

A malaria forecast estimates how many malaria cases are expected in an area, such as a state, over a period of time. It describes the community as a whole and is used for planning and preparedness. It does not describe any one person and cannot show whether you have malaria. Only a malaria test can do that.

Source: bakare-2026-adamawa, who-malaria

## What information are malaria forecasts built from?

Malaria forecasts are usually built from past records of malaria cases together with weather information such as rainfall and temperature. For example, a forecasting study for Adamawa State, Nigeria, used monthly malaria case counts, rainfall and temperature from 2015 to 2024.

Source: bakare-2026-adamawa

## How certain is a malaria forecast?

A forecast is an estimate, not a guarantee. It comes with a range of possible values, and that range gets wider the further ahead the forecast looks. In the Adamawa State study, the forecast for October 2025 was about 80,000 cases, with a range from about 56,000 to about 104,000.

Source: bakare-2026-adamawa

## What can a malaria forecast not take into account?

Forecasts based on weather and past cases cannot fully account for things that change malaria levels for other reasons, such as changes in mosquito control measures, access to health care, or changes in land use and irrigation.

Source: bakare-2026-adamawa

## What does malaria incidence mean?

Malaria incidence is the number of new cases of malaria in a year for every 1,000 people at risk. It is the measure the World Health Organization and the World Bank use to compare malaria levels between places and over time.

Source: worldbank-malaria-incidence

## What does malaria prevalence mean?

Malaria prevalence is the share of people in a group who have malaria parasites in their blood at the time they are tested, for example the percentage of young children who test positive in a survey.

Source: PENDING

## What should I do if the malaria risk in my area is high?

A higher malaria risk in your area is a reason to be extra careful with prevention: sleep under a mosquito net, use repellent after dusk, and get tested promptly if anyone has a fever.

Source: who-malaria

## Does a low malaria risk level mean a fever is not malaria?

No. A low risk level for an area does not rule out malaria in a person. Malaria symptoms are not specific, and getting tested early is important whatever the forecast says.

Source: who-malaria
