## Does malaria always need treatment?

Yes. Malaria is a serious infection and always requires treatment with medicine. Getting treatment early for mild malaria can stop the infection from becoming severe.

Source: who-malaria

## What medicine is recommended for malaria?

Artemisinin-based combination therapies, known as ACTs, are the most effective treatment for P. falciparum malaria, the type most common in Africa. They are the mainstay of the treatment recommended by the World Health Organization. An ACT combines two medicines that work in different ways.

Source: who-malaria, who-malaria-qa

## Who decides which malaria medicine and how much to take?

A doctor or other qualified health worker. Several medicines are used to treat malaria, and health professionals choose based on the type of malaria, whether the parasite is resistant to a medicine, the weight or age of the person, and whether the person is pregnant. Because the right medicine and amount depend on the person, they should come from a health worker and not from guesswork or someone else's leftover medicine.

Source: who-malaria

## Why should malaria medicine only be taken after a positive test?

ACTs are the most effective malaria medicines available today, and no replacement is expected for several years, so they must be kept working. For this reason WHO recommends that malaria treatment is given only if a person tests positive for malaria.

Source: who-malaria-qa

## Why is it important to finish the full course of malaria medicine?

Malaria medicine should be taken exactly as the health worker directs, and the full course should be finished even if the person feels better after a day or two. Stopping early can leave parasites in the body, so the illness may come back.

Source: who-malaria-qa

## Are malaria medicines pills or injections?

Most medicines used for malaria are in pill form. Some people, including those with severe malaria, may need to go to a health centre or hospital for medicines given by injection.

Source: who-malaria

## Do medicines for fever or pain treat malaria?

No. Medicines that bring down a fever or ease pain can help a person feel more comfortable, but they do not kill the malaria parasite. A person with malaria still needs malaria medicine from a health worker.

Source: who-malaria
