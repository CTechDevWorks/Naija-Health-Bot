## What are the first symptoms of malaria?

The most common early symptoms of malaria are fever, headache and chills. These first symptoms may be mild and can look like many other illnesses that cause fever, which makes malaria hard to recognise from symptoms alone.

Source: who-malaria, who-malaria-qa

## How soon after a mosquito bite do malaria symptoms start?

Symptoms of malaria usually start within 10 to 15 days of being bitten by an infected mosquito.

Source: who-malaria

## What other symptoms can come with malaria?

People with malaria may also have sweating, tiredness, body and joint aches, nausea, vomiting or loss of appetite. None of these on its own shows that an illness is malaria.

Source: PENDING

## Can you tell if it is malaria from the symptoms alone?

No. Some malaria symptoms are not specific, which means other illnesses cause them too. A malaria test is the only way to know. Testing lets health workers tell malaria apart from other causes of fever, so that the right treatment is given. Getting tested early is important.

Source: who-malaria, who-malaria-qa

## Can malaria be mild or have no symptoms at all?

Yes. Malaria symptoms may be mild for some people, especially people who have had a malaria infection before. In areas where malaria is common, people who have developed partial immunity may be infected and have no symptoms at all.

Source: who-malaria, who-malaria-qa

## How quickly can malaria become serious?

Malaria caused by P. falciparum, the type most common in Africa, can progress to severe illness and death if it is not treated within 24 hours. WHO recommends prompt diagnosis for anyone with suspected malaria. Getting treatment early for mild malaria can stop the infection from becoming severe.

Source: who-malaria, who-malaria-qa
