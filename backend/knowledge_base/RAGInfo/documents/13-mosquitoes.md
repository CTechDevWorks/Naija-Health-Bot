## Which mosquitoes spread malaria?

Malaria is spread by the bites of infected female Anopheles mosquitoes. There are more than 400 different species of Anopheles mosquito, and around 40 of them can transmit malaria.

Source: who-malaria-qa

## Do Aedes and Culex mosquitoes spread malaria?

No. Mosquitoes such as Aedes and Culex do not spread malaria, although they can spread other diseases. Only Anopheles mosquitoes spread malaria.

Source: who-malaria-qa, plos-anopheles-nigeria

## Where do malaria mosquitoes breed?

Malaria mosquitoes lay their eggs in water, and the young mosquitoes grow there. In Nigeria, most drains and puddles of stagnant water are found to contain Anopheles larvae.

Source: nmep-ivm

## What kinds of water do malaria mosquitoes prefer?

Anopheles mosquitoes commonly breed in shallow, still water open to sunlight, such as puddles, rice fields, open drains, hoof prints, water-filled containers and marshy ground. Warm weather and standing water after rain help them multiply faster.

Source: nmep-ivm, who-lsm-manual

## When do malaria mosquitoes bite?

The mosquitoes that spread malaria in Africa bite mainly at night, from dusk to dawn. Many rest indoors after feeding, while some bite and rest outdoors. This is why sleeping under a net and using repellent after dusk are important.

Source: who-malaria

## How does a mosquito pass malaria to a person?

A mosquito picks up malaria parasites when it bites a person who has malaria. The parasites develop inside the mosquito, and it can then pass them to the next person it bites. This is why malaria spreads through mosquitoes and not directly between people.

Source: who-malaria, who-malaria-qa

## How are malaria mosquitoes monitored in Nigeria?

Nigeria's National Malaria Elimination Programme has set up monitoring sites across the country where mosquitoes are collected regularly. The sites track which mosquito species are present and whether they are becoming resistant to insecticides, and the results are used to decide which insecticide-treated nets to distribute.

Source: nmep-strategic-plan-2021-2025
